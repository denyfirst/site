## What Mohur is

Mohur lets people at organizations that each run their own Mohur server write to each other, end to end encrypted. You need an invite from your organization's admin. The app works only with a Mohur server, which your organization runs, not denyfirst. What each part keeps is on the privacy page.

## How you may use it

Do not use Mohur to:

- harass, threaten, abuse or demean anyone, or send anyone offensive, hateful, violent or sexually explicit content;
- send spam, or messages in bulk that people did not ask for;
- share anything that is illegal where you or the recipient are, including child sexual abuse material, or that infringes someone else's rights;
- spread malware, or try to get into accounts, devices or servers that are not yours;
- pretend to be someone else, or get around the limits your admin or the app sets.

## If someone breaks these terms

- **Block them.** In the conversation, open the menu and choose Block. This happens on your computer only, and they are not told.
- **Report them to your admin.** In the same menu, choose Report to your admin. Your admin sees your name, whom you reported, the reason and your note, never your messages.
- **Your admin acts.** An admin can remove a person from their own organization, choose who may write with each partner, and disconnect a partner altogether.

Messages are encrypted end to end, so no server can read them, and neither can denyfirst or Microsoft. Nobody can review a conversation unless someone in it shows it to them. denyfirst runs no server that you use and cannot remove anyone from an organization. If you believe an organization uses Mohur to break the law, contact that organization or the authorities. Problems with the app itself go to hello@denyfirst.dev.

## Your organization

Your organization's admin invites you, decides whom you may write to, and can remove you. Your organization runs the server you use and answers for it; its own rules and policies apply to it.
